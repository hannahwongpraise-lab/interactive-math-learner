Lab 02 · Algebra

Shape-Shifting Parabolas

In vertex form y = a(x − h)² + k, each letter has a job: a stretches and flips, h slides left–right and k slides up–down. Watch where the parabola crosses the x-axis — those are its roots.

Lab 03 · Geometry

Seeing the Pythagorean Theorem

For any right triangle, the square on the longest side has exactly the same area as the other two squares combined: a² + b² = c². In the second picture, four copies of the triangle slide around inside a big square to prove it — no algebra needed.
